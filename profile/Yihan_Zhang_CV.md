Yihan Zhang. From Recorded Corrections to Oracle Claims: A Measurement Audit for EHR Concept Interventions.

Submitted to the 15th IEEE International Conference on Healthcare Informatics (ICHI 2027), 2026. Sole author.

•  Identified a measurement-validity problem in EHR concept interventions while stress-testing C3B: replacing learned concepts with recorded clinical references measures the model’s response to those records, but does not by itself identify the effect of replacing them with study-defined true concepts.

•  Formalized the observed-reference versus oracle-effect distinction, constructed observationally indistinguishable data-generating worlds with opposite oracle Brier effects, and derived finite-sample certificates using independent error calibration, exact binomial count bounds, and signed worst-case loss allocation.

•  Evaluated the framework on 240 frozen synthetic claims with two prediction heads and audited frozen MIMIC-IV and eICU replays. All 53 strong-effect claims per head were certified in the synthetic evaluation with no observed false approvals; the EHR audits identified the additional measurement evidence required for oracle interpretation.
